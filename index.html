Your mission

Help the cleaner learn

An underwater cleaner needs examples before it can tell sea creatures from rubbish. You will be its teacher.

  1. Listen to a detailed object description.
  2. Label it as ocean life or rubbish.
  3. Test what the model learned from you.

What you’ll discover

Three ideas beneath the surface

Training data

Models learn from labelled examples—not from rules they invent by themselves.

02

Patterns

A model compares features such as shape, material, movement, or colour.

03

Bias & care

Missing or unbalanced examples can produce unfair or unreliable results.
